ChillPOS Portable — macOS
=========================

1) Chon dung zip:
   - Apple Silicon (M1+): ChillPos_POS_Release_macOS_arm64.zip
   - Intel:               ChillPos_POS_Release_macOS_x64.zip
2) Giai nen vao ~/ChillPOS (khong dau cach trong duong dan).
3) Lan dau neu Gatekeeper chan:

   cd ~/ChillPOS
   xattr -cr .
   chmod +x ChillPOS-Start.command ChillPOS-Stop.command start.sh stop.sh
   open ChillPOS-Start.command

4) Web: http://127.0.0.1:8999
5) Tat: ChillPOS-Stop.command

Yeu cau: macOS 14+ 

In an
-----
Khong co USB printer-bridge Windows.
Dung network_tcp (may in LAN) hoac browser_bridge tren thiet bi cam tay.

Kiem tra
--------
Sau khi Start: ./macos-smoke-check.sh

Cong: 8999 (web) / 9990 (PHP) / 33306 (MySQL)
